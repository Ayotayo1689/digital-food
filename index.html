A FEW GOOD THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions,
straight answers.

Where do you deliver?

Delivery options are available across selected areas of Lagos. Enter your address at checkout to see whether delivery is available and what it costs.

How much does delivery cost?

Your delivery fee is shown clearly at checkout before you pay. Qualifying baskets over ₦50,000 are eligible for free delivery in covered areas.

Can I check on my order?

Once your order is placed, you can sign in to see its latest status and order details in your account.
